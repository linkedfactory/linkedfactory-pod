= LinkedFactory Pod
:description: LinkedFactory Pod connects descriptions of industrial assets with their time-series measurements.

LinkedFactory Pod connects descriptions of industrial assets with their measurements.
You describe machines and sensors in RDF, write their measurements over HTTP, and query both together.

Pod is the reference implementation of the https://github.com/linkedfactory/specification[LinkedFactory architecture].
It is built on the semantic platform https://github.com/enilink/enilink[eniLINK].

== How it works

An asset such as `https://example.org/Press/Frame/StrainGauge` appears in two places:

* in an RDF *model*, with its type, label, and relationships to other assets
* in the *KVIN store*, as the item of measurement series such as `p:force`

Both use the same IRI.
A SPARQL query can therefore find the sensor by its description and read its measurements in one step.
xref:explanation:kvin-data-model.adoc[] explains the data model, xref:explanation:architecture.adoc[] the building blocks and their limits.
